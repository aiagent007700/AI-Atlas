# Review queue and publishing policy

Automation should reduce the cost of staying current, not remove judgment from the system. The queue separates **discovery** from **interpretation**.

## Route items by risk

| Route | Typical item | Publication behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Primary-source metadata and a link | May publish automatically |
| Amber | A technical result needing context | Publish as discovery; review before synthesis |
| Red | Quote, forecast, controversy, or commercial claim | Hold for review |

## Review checklist

Before promoting an item into a tutorial chapter or industry-voice block, check:

- Is the source primary or does it link to the primary source?
- Does the wording preserve the source's scope and uncertainty?
- Are numbers accompanied by a baseline, date, and evaluation setting?
- Is a quote short, exact, attributable, and in context?
- Does the update add understanding rather than repeat the headline?
- Is the relevant durable chapter linked?

## Suggested pull-request labels

- `auto-discovery`
- `needs-context`
- `quote-check`
- `standards-watch`
- `industry-claim`
- `correction`

## Operating rhythm

- **Daily:** collect and publish traceable discoveries.
- **Weekly:** synthesize recurring themes and promote the best items.
- **Monthly:** prune duplicates, repair links, and revise the taxonomy.
